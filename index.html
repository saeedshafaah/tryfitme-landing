Why FitMe?

Size issues are one of the biggest reasons for fashion returns. FitMe combines size recommendation with visual confidence.

How it works

Customers enter height, weight, and fit preference → FitMe recommends the best size → customers see themselves wearing the product → they buy with confidence.

What merchants gain

Fewer returns, higher conversion, fewer “Which size should I choose?” messages, and easy Shopify integration.
